What to wear

Indian formal if you have it, black tie if you do not, and comfortable shoes for the ceremony either way — the courtyard is uneven stone. February evenings by the lake get genuinely cold, so bring a shawl you like being photographed in.

Good to know

Arrival
The nearest airport is Maharana Pratap, 30 minutes from the old city.
Children
Very welcome at every event except the sangeet.
Parking
Free at both hotels. There is none at the palace — take the shuttle.
Weather
24°C by day, 11°C after dark. Every year, without fail.
